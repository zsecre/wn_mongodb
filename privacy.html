Privacy Policy for WatchNinja

Last Updated: September 29, 2026 | Effective Date: September 2026

Welcome to WatchNinja ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application WatchNinja collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our app and services.

Summary: WatchNinja does not require registration or personal account creation. Your watchlists, history, and app preferences are stored securely on your local device.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. When you use WatchNinja, we may access the following data:

  • Local Data & Preferences: Your profile nickname, selected avatar, custom watchlist, playback progress, and viewing history are stored strictly locally on your device using Android Room and SharedPreferences.
  • Device & Network State: Non-personal technical information such as device type, Android OS version, and network connectivity status (used solely to maintain smooth media streaming and display network connection alerts).
  • Anonymous Statistics & Usage: Anonymous view counts and ratings to aggregate community engagement. No personal identifying information (PII) is attached to these statistics.

2. Third-Party Services & APIs

WatchNinja utilizes trusted third-party services to deliver metadata, stream catalogs, and ads:

  • The Movie Database (TMDb): Provides titles, synopsis, cast lists, and poster artwork.
  • Jikan / MyAnimeList API: Provides seasonal anime lists, schedules, and information.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads: Displays non-intrusive banner, native, and interstitial advertisements to keep WatchNinja free for everyone. Google AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers (AAID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Services: Used for anonymous community view statistics, notifications, and remote update notices.

3. Local Storage & Permissions

WatchNinja requires minimal permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch media metadata and video streams.
  • VIBRATE & POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used for optional update notices and UI feedback.
  • WAKE_LOCK & FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Used during fullscreen video playback to prevent the screen from turning off while you watch.

4. User Data Control & Deletion

You have full control over your stored data in WatchNinja at any time:

  • Clear Media Cache: You can clear temporary streaming buffers and image cache at any time inside the Me / Profile section of the app.
  • Reset Application: You can reset all local history, watchlists, and profile settings with a single tap inside the app's settings.
  • Uninstalling the App: Deleting WatchNinja from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes all stored data.

5. Children's Privacy

WatchNinja does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
